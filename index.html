About Me

Hey, I'm Jeremy, a third-year Computer Science student at Wilfrid Laurier University with a deep passion for cybersecurity. I've spent the past two years building real security skills through hands-on home labs, replicating the exact tools and workflows used by actual security analysts.

My focus is on the defensive side of security, SIEM monitoring, threat detection, cloud security, and vulnerability assessment. I care about understanding why systems fail, not just that they do. That curiosity drives everything I build.

I'm actively pursuing my CompTIA Network+ and Security+ certifications, and I'm looking for co-op or internship opportunities where I can contribute to a real security team.
